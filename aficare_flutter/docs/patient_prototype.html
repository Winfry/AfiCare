How this works

Age is calculated from DOB and compared against the hardcoded Kenya EPI schedule, then combined with saved vaccination records to show due/overdue status. For a child profile this is the main screen guardians use.

Health Tool

Drug Checker

A static interaction reference — not a live clinical database.

How this works

No backend, no persistence. The current implementation is a hardcoded interaction table. It should never imply that it replaces a pharmacist or clinician.

Clinical

Adherence Log

See which doses were taken, skipped or missed.

How this works

Prescription + adherence data are real patient data (see Medications). Each scheduled dose becomes a log entry the patient marks taken or skipped, and marking one here or on Medications updates Home too. Prototype assumption: a dose left unmarked past its time counts as missed.

Your care

Share Records

Choose what leaves your record, who can open it, and for how long.

How this works

Prototype design: sharing creates a time-limited, revocable grant scoped to the categories you pick, and the QR carries a share token, not your records. Scanning a facility's QR checks you in and shares only what you approve. The QR graphics here are illustrative and not scannable; camera scanning is optional and may not work in every viewer.

Your care

Care Team

People involved in your care.

How this works

CareTeamProvider supplies the patient's care-team relationships. Provider photos should be used here when available; a fallback avatar should be used when no photo exists. Home, Appointments and this screen all read the same CareTeamProvider list, so no new backend is needed.

Identity

Profile & MediLink

Your personal information, AfiCare identity and the family profiles you manage.

How this works

DependentProvider supplies the guardian's dependent profiles (stored in dependent_profiles, IDs prefixed ML-DEP). Switching sets the active patient for every other screen, so appointments, medications, records and the rest all re-read for whoever is active. Prototype assumption: adult-only tools (mental health, maternal care, men's health, caregiver access) are hidden while a child is active.
